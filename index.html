Practical Guide

1. Off-Chain Direct Liquidity No central escrow. All transfers are peer-to-peer via mobile money. Platform never holds funds.
2. Phantom Compounding + FIFO Equity grows as S(t) = P · (1+r)^t. Liquidation requires a matching new buyer in the sell queue.
3. Saturation Threshold Sustainable cash-outs at rate r require recruit volume to grow ≈ V(t+1) = V(t)·(1+r). When population growth slows, Twait → ∞ and the system stalls.
4. Agent-Based Dynamics New agents arrive stochastically. Viral coefficient multiplies inflow when existing agents recruit. Monte Carlo runs quantify survival probability under noise.
